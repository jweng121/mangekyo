     1. Scope of representation
     Client retains the firm for advice and representation in the matter identified above. The firm will keep the client informed

     2. Compensation and calculation of fees
       If recovery is obtained before a complaint is filed, the attorney fee is thirty-three percent (33%) of the client's gross
     recovery. Once a complaint is filed, the fee is forty percent (40%) of gross recovery, including a settlement reached after
      filing and before trial. Filing suit is the event that changes the tier; the fee is not reduced because trial does not occur.
     Gross recovery is measured before costs and liens.
     3. Costs and shared recoveries
      Filing fees, expert charges, records and reporting expenses are accounted for separately. In a jointly represented matter, a
     signed allocation agreement controls each client's share of the recovery and shared costs. Each client retains a separate
      trust ledger and this individual fee agreement.
     4. Statements and client authorization
     Before a settlement distribution, the firm will provide a statement showing the client allocation, fee basis, costs, liens and
     net proceeds. Client funds remain in trust until earned fees or authorized expenses are paid. Questions about a statement
     should be directed to the responsible attorney before approval.
